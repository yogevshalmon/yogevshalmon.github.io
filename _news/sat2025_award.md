<b>Best Student Paper Award at SAT 2025!</b>

Our paper <em>Enumerating All Boolean Matches</em> (with Alexander Nadel) received the <b>Best Student Paper Award</b> at the 28th International Conference on Theory and Applications of Satisfiability Testing (SAT 2025). The companion open-source tool <a href="https://github.com/yogevshalmon/ebat" target="_blank">EBat</a> is publicly available.
