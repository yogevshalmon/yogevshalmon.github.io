<b>Introducing GPAP: Boolean constraint propagation on the GPU!</b>

<a href="https://github.com/yogevshalmon/gpap" target="_blank">GPAP</a> propagates thousands of partial assignments at once, in a single kernel launch, and is now open source. Our preprint <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28805" target="_blank"><em>SAT Sampling at Scale via GPU-based Boolean Constraint Propagation</em></a> (with Alexander Nadel and Ofer Strichman) builds a SAT sampler on top of it.
