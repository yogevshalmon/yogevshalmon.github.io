I am a PhD student in the [Faculty of Data and Decision Sciences](https://dds.technion.ac.il/) at the [Technion — Israel Institute of Technology](https://www.technion.ac.il/), advised by [Alexander Nadel](https://dblp.org/pid/07/2593.html) and [Ofer Strichman](https://oferstrichman.net.technion.ac.il/).

My research focuses on **SAT solvers** and their applications. These days I am building **[GPAP](https://github.com/yogevshalmon/gpap)**, a GPU engine for Boolean constraint propagation:

{% include gpap_card.liquid %}

I am currently a **PhD Student Researcher at NVIDIA**, working in Formal Verification Development alongside my PhD — not a summer internship, but an ongoing student position. Previously, I worked as a research student at **Intel Corporation** in Haifa, Israel, where my work on Boolean matching ([EBat](https://github.com/yogevshalmon/ebat)) was productized as part of Intel's library mapping flow. My work has been published at the International Conference on Theory and Applications of Satisfiability Testing (**SAT**) and at Formal Methods in Computer-Aided Design (**FMCAD**), and received the **Best Student Paper Award** at SAT 2025.
